Privacy Policy - Big Word

Last updated: January 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Big Word app (“App”) handles user information.

1. Data collection

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account creation, login, registration form, or transmission of user information to any server.

2. User-entered content (texts)

Text entered in the App is used only for the main functionality (displaying text fullscreen). This content is not sent to us and is not collected by any external service.

Depending on the operating system and device, the content may be saved locally on the user’s device (e.g., app local storage) so you can reuse texts later. When present, this storage exists only on the device.

3. Permissions

The App does not request sensitive permissions (such as location, contacts, camera, or microphone). If an Internet permission appears by default due to platform/build configuration, it is not used for collecting user data.

4. Third-party services

The App does not use third-party services for analytics, advertising, tracking, or user data collection (for example, Firebase Analytics, ads SDKs, etc.).

5. Data sharing

Since we do not collect personal data, we do not share user information with third parties.

6. Security

As a rule, we do not process data on servers. Any local data remains on the user’s device and is protected by the device and operating system security mechanisms.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children and does not collect personal data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect App improvements or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date will be adjusted when changes are made.
